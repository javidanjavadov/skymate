Know the difference between a reading and a prediction

Most weather apps show a model estimate and call it "current". SkyMate shows you the nearest real instrument reading, how far away it is and how old it is, and labels every estimate as an estimate.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where does SkyMate's data come from?

Measurements come from airport weather stations and national weather services worldwide. Forecasts come from global weather models run by NOAA (United States) and ECMWF (Europe). SkyMate stores its own copy of both.

What's the difference between "measured" and a forecast?

A measurement is a reading from a physical instrument at a weather station. A forecast is a model's calculation. SkyMate always tells you which one you're looking at, and how old it is.

How do I pay for Premium?

Inside Telegram, with Telegram Stars. Open the bot, tap ⭐ Premium, and confirm. The subscription renews monthly and you can cancel anytime in Telegram under Settings → My Stars.

Can I use SkyMate in my own product?

Yes. The Weather API has free and paid plans for developers and businesses. See the Weather API tab under Pricing and the API documentation.

How accurate are the forecasts?

No forecast is perfect. Temperatures for today and tomorrow are typically within 1–2 °C; accuracy decreases gradually toward day 10. For what's happening right now, SkyMate shows real measurements.

What do you do with my data?

Only what's needed to run the service. You can delete your data anytime with /deletemydata in the bot. Read the Privacy Policy.
